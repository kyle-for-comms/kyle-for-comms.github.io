The plan

Running for IOP Communications Director. Three commitments, fifteen specific things I'll do.

1

Coverage

The IOP has 17 programs, but five were never mentioned in our weekly newsletters. A stronger reputation starts with showcasing all our work and talent.

  • The Coverage LogKeep a public log of programs featured in every email and post. If I skip someone, you'll see it.
  • RotationFeature two programs in each email so every part of the IOP gets its turn in the spotlight.
  • Pubbing EverywhereShare IOP events across Houses and affinity groups, including Director's Internships and the One Fund.
  • Match Rejection with SuggestionSuggest three other programs students can join that week, including the coalitions.
  • SnapshotsContinuing to ensure all IOP events always have a communications representative to document the moment.
2

Creativity

Our communications need to be fun and engaging. I want to reinvigorate our videos and newsletters with fresh faces and new formats, and draw new people to the IOP.

  • Faces in Every EmailReal members at real events in every newsletter to showcase our community.
  • New Video FormatsBeyond the weekly recap: 60-second explainers, a day inside a program, skits, and interviews with IOP members.
  • Program Leads on CameraProgram leads in communications videos across the IOP to highlight unique experiences.
  • Supporting Student JournalismLead every Monday newsletter with HPR's pick of the previous week.
  • The IOP YearbookCapture all the memories through a digital yearbook and highlight memories from the IOP past, present, and future!
  • PodcastsWant to hear from speakers, students, and mentors? Tune into podcasts with members and guests of the IOP to listen to discussions on what YOU want to know!
3

Accountability

The only way to know we're moving in the right direction is to measure how people react to us. That's why we need accountability for promises made.

  • Public SAC MinutesPublished within a week of every meeting. This is a student organization, not the National Security Council.
  • The State of the IOPA twice-a-semester email with poll findings and program metrics.
  • HPR Polls Every SemesterRun the same public opinion poll every semester and publish the results, whatever they say.
  • IOP Operating ManualExplain who to contact and how to submit your own proposal to SAC.
