The plan

Fifteen things I'll do as Comms Director, grouped into three bricks: Coverage, Creativity, and Accountability. Click any brick to read more.

1

Coverage

The IOP has 17 programs, but five were never mentioned in our weekly newsletters. A stronger reputation starts with showcasing all our work and talent.

  • The Coverage LogKeep a public log of programs featured in every email and post. If I skip someone, you'll see it.At the end of the semester, we will share a graph with everyone showing how we did so the student body can evaluate us.
  • RotationFeature two programs in each email so every part of the IOP gets its turn in the spotlight.By the end, every program should be mentioned twice.
  • Pubbing EverywhereShare IOP events across Houses and affinity groups, including Director's Internships and the One Fund.We need to reach more people and more places so everyone, not just politics nerds, feels welcome in the IOP.
  • Match Rejection with SuggestionIf someone gets rejected from a selective program, suggest three other programs students can join that week, including the coalitions.This will both help keep people in the IOP and can grow membership of the less represented groups.
  • SnapshotsContinuing to ensure all IOP events always have a communications representative to document the moment.This way we never miss a beat.
2

Creativity

Our communications need to be fun and engaging. I want to reinvigorate our videos and newsletters with fresh faces and new formats, and draw new people to the IOP.

  • Faces in Every EmailReal members at real events in every newsletter to showcase our community.This way you see us and not just graphics.
  • New Video FormatsBeyond the weekly recap: 60-second explainers, a day inside a program, skits, and interviews with IOP members.I want to revolutionize the way we explain ourselves to the Harvard student body.
  • Program Leads on CameraProgram leads in communications videos across the IOP to highlight unique experiences.I want the community to know who is leading our amazing programs so that everyone feels appreciated and younger members can be inspired to one day lead a program themselves.
  • Supporting Student JournalismLead every Monday newsletter with HPR's pick of the previous week.This way we can help to showcase our amazing writers and also can aid in informing IOP members of what is going on in the country and the world.
  • The IOP YearbookCapture all the memories through a digital yearbook and highlight memories from the IOP past, present, and future!Then you'll be able to share memories throughout the year.
  • PodcastsWant to hear from speakers, students, and mentors? Tune into podcasts with members and guests of the IOP to listen to discussions on what YOU want to know.
3

Accountability

The only way to know we're moving in the right direction is to measure how people react to us. That's why we need accountability for promises made.

  • Public SAC MinutesPublished within a week of every meeting. This is a student organization, not the National Security Council.I want people to feel like they know why decisions are being made and where people stand on issues so we can finally get rid of SAC's reputation for opacity.
  • The State of the IOPA twice-a-semester email with poll findings and program metrics.Through this email, people will know where we were, where we are, and where we are going next.
  • HPR Polls Every SemesterRun the same public opinion poll every semester and publish the results, whatever they say.I will work with HPR leadership to make sure this happens, both for our own understanding of our work's perception and a broader mission to aid in transparent communication.
  • IOP Operating ManualExplain who to contact and how to submit your own proposal to SAC.Most people in the IOP don't know this is something they can do, and that shouldn't be the case.
