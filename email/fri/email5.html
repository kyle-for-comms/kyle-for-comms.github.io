Hi IOP,

On Monday I said I'd stop describing a better IOP newsletter and send you one instead. This week you got three issues, a few new features on my website, and a closer look at what I stand for.

But I realized I never actually introduced myself. So tonight, I want to change that.

Here's the short version. I walked into the IOP with big hopes for what I wanted to do with my life: serve others and make the world just that much better. What I found was a community of people who wanted the same thing, but some of them got discouraged by the gap between their own compass and the direction of the organization.

I'm running to close that gap, and to make the IOP the hub of public service it can and should be.
