The platform
THE PROBLEM
Every newsletter looks the same, so the best stuff inside gets skimmed past.
Meet people where they already are.

The IOP already has a website with an events calendar, and the weekly IOP newsletter reaches every member’s inbox. We don’t need more places to put information. We need communications people actually want to open. That’s what Creativity is about: real members in every issue, short videos, and new formats that make the newsletter something you look forward to, not something you scroll past.

WHAT I'D DO
1Faces in every email. Real members at real events. No more faceless IOP.
2New video formats. Explainers, a day inside a program, skits, interviews.
3Program leads on camera. The people who run things, explaining them.
4HPR first. Every Monday newsletter leads with HPR's pick.
5Open the doors. Fellows' office hours and our best Forums, promoted beyond the IOP to Houses, clubs and classes, so students who've never been here have an easy first step in.
